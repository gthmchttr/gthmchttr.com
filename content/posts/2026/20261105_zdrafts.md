I vibe coded a [WYSIWYG editor for Zola static site generator](https://zdrafts.gthmchttr.com), that has helped me tremendously while writing this and many other posts.

You see I have a problem - major part of my waking hours is me not having access to my laptop.

On the other side, I own a phone that pretty much stays with me 24/7 - no I am not always on it, even though that may have made me look like a phone-addict right now.

The point being, a markdown based static site generator, that requires you to write content on a text editor on a computer is... not a very good way for someone trying to maintain consistency.

One may argue, this gives you a good separation of concerns - blog only when you are fully committed to write one. Which I agree would help me put out a more well thought-out content. 

That’s simply not what I am going for. 

**I just want to get my thoughts out!**

I want this to become like twitter to me - got a cool idea? 

1. Jot it down real quick, with formatting being part of writing. 
2. Publish it to the world!

Just 2 simple steps instead of,

1. Write to a notes app on phone.
2. Check if it synced to my laptop (once I have access to one)
3. Copy content over to a markdown, and make any formatting changes. 
4. Publish and hope, things still look as I intended them to. 

Also, realistically speaking, as a grown man with a family - I only have so much time I can spend on my personal endeavours. And using every little free time counts.

So, making a quick post like this on my lunch break, commute, or simply being able to do it whenever and wherever I want is a huge plus.

Honestly speaking, there are a ton of other ways I could have done it, 

- Medium, Dev.to, Wordpress, \<insert your favourite CMS here> - No because, I did not want a ecosystem lock-in!
- bearblog, mataroa, neocities - a solid alternative, but i love to host it myself.
- Twitter (X), Facebook, LinkedIn - nah, nope, never... I’m not giving up my freedom for an audience. 

This turned out more of a rant than an explainer of what zdrafts actually is. Well that’s a topic for another day.

For now go [try](https://zdrafts.gthmchttr.com/) it out, give a [star](https://github.com/gthmchttr/zdrafts) if you like it.
