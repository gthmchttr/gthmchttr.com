## Let's see that Motherboard

As much as I tried to avoid doing it, this turned out to be more important than I had initially assumed. Also this was the primary reason why my first attempt failed. 

You see, every R36S is not born equal, it may look same on outside but it's what inside that matters! I mean the motherboard...

![R36S Motherboard](/202610/r36s_motherboard.JPG)

**R36S-V30 2025-11-18** - this is what you need.

Any OS you flash, needs to know configuration of its hardware. This is done using {{ <color text=".dtb" color="blue" /> }} files. You'll find them in the root folder of your memory card -

![]()

{{ <color text=".dtb" color="blue" /> }} includes config like what screen is being used, configuration and layout of physical buttons, etc. This will be used by OS to load drivers and map correct button to correct action. 

Opening up your back panel also helps in knowing if your device is genuine or a clone. There’s a {{ <elink text="whoooole" href="https://handhelds.wiki/R36S_Overview#R36S-V30_2025-11-18" /> }} lot of motherboards that you can compare with and confirm your variant! 

## Choose your OS

I did very little research on this, as I was clear from get go what OS I wanted to install. There's actually 3 major options out there,

- {{ <elink text="ArkOS" href="https://github.com/christianhaitian/arkos" /> }} / {{ <elink text="ArkOS For Clones" href="https://github.com/lcdyk0517/arkos4clone" /> }} - the OG, most stable and compatible with almost every handheld out there. 
- {{ <elink text="dArkOSen" href="https://github.com/djparentx/dArkOSen-R36S" /> }} / {{ <elink text="dArkOSRE" href="https://github.com/southoz/dArkOSRE-R36" /> }} - built on top of ArkOS, has stability of ArkOS with a more modern core. 
- {{ <elink text="ROCKNIX" href="https://github.com/ROCKNIX/distribution" /> }} - a more modern alternative which would be a good option few years down the line.

I choose you {{ <shimmer text="dArkOSRE" /> }}...

<u>Why dArkOS</u>? - ArkOS is not maintained anymore, so dArkOS it is!  
<u>Why dArkOSRE</u>? - RE version is the one optimized for R36S handlheld.  
<u>Why not ROCKNIX?</u> - I did not hear of this one when I started, might be a good future option I guess.  

Once you are clear what needs to be installed, it's time to download the image. You can find dArkOSRE version {{ <elink text="here" href="https://github.com/southoz/dArkOSRE-R36/releases" /> }}.

Next, you also need a tool to flash it on a memory card, I choose {{ <elink text="Raspberry Pi Imager" href="https://www.raspberrypi.com/software/" /> }} that just works fine. 

Once you have both of them downloaded it's time to...
